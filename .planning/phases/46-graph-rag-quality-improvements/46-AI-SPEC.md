## 1. System Classification

**System Type:** RAG

**Description:**
ContextOS retrieves source-backed decision memory from tickets, source evidence, graph neighborhoods, and related work so context pages and future suggestions can explain why an answer or action recommendation is grounded. Good output is measurable, workspace-scoped, provenance-rich, fresh enough for the user's task, and explicit about which source and graph signals supported the result.

**Critical Failure Modes:**
1. A context page or suggestion cites unsupported or fabricated decision evidence.
2. Retrieval ranks stale, synthesized, or unverified context above fresher source-backed evidence without warning.
3. Graph traversal crosses workspace boundaries or uses unrelated graph neighborhoods as grounding.
4. Suggestion/action grounding produces user-visible recommendations without exact source anchors and explainable retrieval signals.
5. Evaluation claims quality improvements without deterministic fixtures for recall, source-link accuracy, freshness, and action-suggestion grounding.

## 1b. Domain Context

> Researched by `gsd-domain-researcher`. Grounds the evaluation strategy in domain expert knowledge.

**Industry Vertical:** B2B developer/product knowledge management and decision intelligence.

**User Population:** Senior engineers, product/design leads, workspace admins, source owners, and future bot/action reviewers using ContextOS context pages, Decision Receipts, Needs review suggestions, and source-backed action prompts.

**Stakes Level:** High

**Output Consequence:** Retrieved evidence changes how teams understand prior decisions, plan implementation work, document missing rationale, and decide whether a suggested follow-up is worth human review. Bad retrieval can waste engineering time, misstate why a decision exists, surface noisy suggestions, leak workspace data, or prepare an ungrounded future action for a human to approve.

### What Domain Experts Evaluate Against

Dimension: Decision evidence precision
Good (domain expert would accept): A context page or suggestion names the specific Decision Receipt or source item, source type, source owner/person when available, stable link, and exact quote/snippet; it separates original evidence from generated context and every decision claim can be traced back to source-backed evidence.
Bad (domain expert would flag): The output says "Slack said", "the graph shows", or "the team decided" without exact source anchors; cites a generated context page as primary evidence; blends adjacent graph facts into a decision nobody actually made.
Stakes: Critical
Source: GRAG-01/GRAG-04 local requirements; EnterpriseRAG-Bench internal-knowledge benchmark categories for Slack/Linear/GitHub/Jira, conflict resolution, and absent-information handling; RAG failure research on source-linking and operational validation.

Dimension: Source freshness and supersession
Good (domain expert would accept): Current confirmed Decision nodes, recent source records, and source-backed promoted candidates outrank stale docs, old conversations, and synthesized context unless the user explicitly asks for history; stale, superseded, or unknown-freshness evidence is labeled and explained.
Bad (domain expert would flag): An old Slack thread or generated context page overrides a newer PR/spec/confirmed Decision without warning; the answer hides that a source is stale, disconnected, soft-deleted, or superseded.
Stakes: High
Source: GRAG-02/GRAG-03 local requirements; OWASP vector/embedding weakness guidance on cross-context knowledge conflict and permission-aware retrieval; Phase 46 research on freshness-aware ranking.

Dimension: Workspace isolation and access parity
Good (domain expert would accept): Every retrieval unit, graph path, citation, suggestion, and trace is scoped to the active workspace and allowed source state; graph traversal never crosses workspaces, and final citations only reference sources valid for the active workspace.
Bad (domain expert would flag): Shared embeddings, graph traversal, or cached context returns another workspace's Slack/docs/tickets, a disconnected integration's records, or a source that should not be visible in the current workspace.
Stakes: Critical
Source: WS-03, GRAG-03, and "cross-workspace graph search" out-of-scope constraints; GDPR integrity/confidentiality and security-of-processing principles; OWASP permission-aware vector store guidance.

Dimension: Action-grounding usefulness
Good (domain expert would accept): A suggestion or future action prompt states the concrete target, source-backed reason, owner/affected system when available, unresolved gap, confidence/freshness state, and why a human should review it; no external write is implied before explicit confirmation.
Bad (domain expert would flag): Generic "create a task", "update docs", or "follow up" text appears from loose similarity or graph adjacency alone; the suggestion lacks a target, source quote/link, owner, or explanation of why the action follows from the evidence.
Stakes: High
Source: ACTL-02/ACTL-03 human-confirmation and audit-trail requirements; Phase 44/45 suggestion and missed-decision contracts; OWASP excessive-agency and prompt-injection guidance.

Dimension: Noise and false-positive control
Good (domain expert would accept): Ambiguous, duplicate, already-resolved, weak-evidence, or low-actionability candidates are suppressed with a reason; the Needs review surface stays bounded and favors fewer, well-evidenced suggestions over broad coverage.
Bad (domain expert would flag): Routine chatter, repeated mentions, stale graph neighbors, already-dismissed suggestions, or incomplete source snippets create visible missed-decision/action suggestions; reviewers stop trusting the queue.
Stakes: High
Source: Phase 44 preservation/dismissal rules; Phase 45 exact-quote/source-link candidate requirements; enterprise RAG research recommending human-in-the-lead evaluation for novel internal questions.

### Known Failure Modes in This Domain

- Citation/provenance drift: generated context, graph-neighbor text, or a related ticket is cited as if it were the original Slack/doc/PR evidence for a decision.
- Stale evidence resurrection: old docs, obsolete Slack threads, or prior context pages outrank newer source-backed Decisions or implementation records without a stale/superseded warning.
- Workspace or source-boundary leakage: shared embeddings, graph traversal, cached retrieval units, or suggestion refresh logic returns sources from the wrong workspace, disconnected integration, or wrong source owner boundary.
- Source-text instruction poisoning: untrusted Slack/docs/tickets contain prompt-like instructions that influence extraction, retrieval explanations, or suggestions instead of being treated as evidence only.
- Suggestion fatigue: weak graph adjacency or incomplete source coverage turns into noisy Needs review items, causing senior reviewers to dismiss the surface instead of using it as a decision-memory quality gate.

### Regulatory / Compliance Context

No sector-specific regulatory regime was identified for this deployment context: this is not healthcare, finance, legal advice, education, or customer-support automation. The directly relevant constraints are privacy, confidentiality, workspace tenancy, and enterprise auditability:

- GDPR / UK GDPR may apply when Slack messages, docs, PR comments, meeting transcripts, names, emails, or other employee/customer personal data are processed for EU/UK data subjects. Evaluation should check purpose limitation, data minimization, storage limitation, integrity/confidentiality, and security of processing.
- B2B security review will likely expect SOC 2-style evidence around security, confidentiality, privacy, availability, and processing integrity, even if SOC 2 is a customer assurance framework rather than a law.
- OWASP GenAI risks are directly relevant because ContextOS uses RAG over proprietary workspace data. Eval fixtures should include prompt-injection, sensitive-information disclosure, vector/embedding leakage, data poisoning, and excessive-agency negative cases.
- Phase 46 must not implement autonomous writes. Action suggestions can be grounded and explained, but Phase 47 owns human-confirmed bot actions and external writes.

## 2. Framework Decision

**Selected Framework:** Existing FastAPI/SQLModel/Postgres service layer

**Version:** No new AI framework for Phase 46. LangGraph 1.0.9 remains an installed dependency but is optional and not required by this contract.

**Rationale:**
Phase 46 is not blocked on an AI orchestration framework. The work is retrieval quality: target routing, deterministic evals, provenance, freshness, ranking features, stream parity, and explanation payloads. Those concerns are already owned by typed FastAPI services, SQLModel/Postgres persistence, pgvector search, `traverse_graph()`, `LLMClient`, and existing Langfuse/Laminar tracing. Adding a framework wrapper now would risk making the planner invent orchestration files before the service contracts exist.

**Alternatives Considered:**

| Framework | Ruled Out Because |
|-----------|------------------|
| LlamaIndex | Strong generic RAG fit, but would add a new framework dependency and duplicate current custom Postgres/pgvector/graph retrieval primitives. |
| LangGraph | Already installed and may be useful later as a thin wrapper, but Phase 46 does not need it to deliver retrieval targets, ranking features, evals, provenance, or stream parity. Use only if a later implementation proves a state graph removes real complexity. |
| OpenAI Agents SDK | Adds provider coupling and does not match the model-agnostic constraint for Phase 46. |
| CrewAI / AutoGen / Google ADK | Multi-agent orchestration is outside Phase 46 scope and would add unnecessary framework surface. |

**Vendor Lock-In Accepted:** No — the contract preserves existing OpenAI/Gemini flexibility and keeps provider-specific calls behind current service wrappers.

### Entry Point Pattern
```python
from __future__ import annotations

from enum import StrEnum
from typing import Literal
from uuid import UUID

from pydantic import BaseModel, Field
from sqlalchemy.ext.asyncio import AsyncSession


class RetrievalTarget(StrEnum):
    DECISION_RECEIPTS = "decision_receipts"
    SOURCE_EVIDENCE = "source_evidence"
    GRAPH_NEIGHBORHOOD = "graph_neighborhood"
    RELATED_WORK = "related_work"


class RetrievalFeatures(BaseModel):
    explicit_ref: bool = False
    rrf_score: float | None = None
    graph_depth: int | None = None
    source_freshness_days: int | None = None
    verification_state: Literal[
        "confirmed_decision",
        "source_backed_candidate",
        "source_evidence",
        "generated_context",
        "unknown",
    ] = "unknown"
    workspace_match: bool = True


class RetrievalTraceItem(BaseModel):
    source_type: str
    source_id: UUID
    title: str
    url: str | None = None
    quote: str | None = None
    targets: list[RetrievalTarget]
    signals: list[str]
    features: RetrievalFeatures
    why_selected: str | None = None
    suppress_reason: str | None = None


class GraphRagResult(BaseModel):
    selected: list[RetrievalTraceItem]
    dropped: list[RetrievalTraceItem] = Field(default_factory=list)
    validation_errors: list[str] = Field(default_factory=list)


async def retrieve_graph_rag_context(
    session: AsyncSession,
    workspace_id: UUID,
    ticket: object,
    targets: list[RetrievalTarget] | None = None,
    token_budget: int = 25_000,
) -> GraphRagResult:
    """Service-layer entry point consumed by context pages, suggestions, and evals."""
    from src.services.linking import find_related_content

    requested_targets = targets or list(RetrievalTarget)
    result = await find_related_content(
        session,
        workspace_id,
        ticket,
    )
    selected: list[RetrievalTraceItem] = []
    errors: list[str] = []
    for item in result.items:
        if not item.signals:
            errors.append(f"{item.source_type}:{item.source_id} has no retrieval signal")
        selected.append(
            RetrievalTraceItem(
                source_type=item.source_type,
                source_id=item.source_id,
                title=item.title,
                url=item.url,
                targets=requested_targets,
                signals=list(item.signals),
                features=RetrievalFeatures(rrf_score=item.relevance_score),
                why_selected=f"Selected by {', '.join(item.signals) or 'unknown signal'}",
            )
        )
    return GraphRagResult(selected=selected, validation_errors=errors)
```

### Key Abstractions
| Concept | What It Is | When You Use It |
|---------|-----------|-----------------|
| `RetrievalTarget` | The explicit retrieval mode requested by a call site: decision receipts, source evidence, graph neighborhood, or related work. | Use whenever context pages, suggestions, evals, or future MCP tools ask for retrieval. |
| `RetrievalTraceItem` | A typed result/explanation unit with source identity, signals, features, and selection/suppression reason. | Use in `context_page.sources`, suggestion payloads, and eval outputs so provenance is inspectable. |
| `RetrievalFeatures` | Ranking/explanation features such as RRF score, graph depth, source freshness, verification state, and workspace match. | Use before final ranking and when explaining why a result won or was dropped. |
| `GraphRagResult` | The selected/dropped result set plus validation errors. | Use as the stable service return type for context generation, suggestion grounding, and eval runners. |
| Deterministic eval case | JSONL fixture with expected source IDs, decision IDs, source links, quote substrings, stale constraints, and must-not-include items. | Use as the pass/fail oracle before tuning ranking or prompts. |

### Common Pitfalls
1. **Adding orchestration before contracts.** Do not create a workflow wrapper until retrieval targets, trace payloads, ranking features, validators, and eval fixtures exist.
2. **Treating generated context as primary evidence.** Generated context pages can help navigation, but primary grounding must prefer original source records or confirmed Decision Receipts.
3. **Hiding ranking features.** A final score without graph path, freshness, verification state, and selected/dropped reasons is not enough for Phase 46.
4. **Diverging stream and non-stream retrieval.** Streaming context generation must use the same target-aware retrieval policy as non-stream generation or record an explicit omission reason.
5. **Streaming partial JSON into validators.** Stream progress for UX, but wait for final structured outputs before Pydantic validation.

## 4. Implementation Guidance

**Model Configuration:**

Use the current model-agnostic wrapper, not direct provider calls inside retrieval/ranking services.

| Use | Model Path | Params |
|-----|------------|--------|
| Context synthesis | Existing `get_llm_client()` default `gemini-3-flash-preview` unless config overrides it | `temperature=0.1-0.2`, explicit `max_output_tokens=4096-8192` after adding that option to `LLMClient.generate()` |
| Retrieval routing / target classification | Prefer deterministic rules first; if LLM is needed, call `LLMClient.generate_json()` | `temperature=0.0`, `max_output_tokens=512`, Pydantic schema required |
| Rerank / evidence validation | Existing `_llm_rerank()` shape, upgraded to structured JSON if changed | `temperature=0.0`, `top_n<=30`, log candidate IDs and validation failures |
| Cheap sub-tasks | Current provider's cheaper configured model only behind `LLMClient` | Use for routing, summarisation, duplicate classification; never for final unsupported claims |

Current gap: `LLMClient.generate()` and `generate_json()` do not expose `max_output_tokens` yet, although `google.genai.types.GenerateContentConfig` supports it. Phase 46 should add this optional parameter before increasing LLM usage.

**Core Pattern:**

Phase 46 should start as a plain async service workflow. The service carries IDs, targets, compact evidence, ranking features, validation notes, and trace payloads; existing modules continue to own database queries, graph traversal, RRF, token trimming, synthesis, and persistence.

```python
from __future__ import annotations

from enum import StrEnum
from uuid import UUID

from pydantic import BaseModel, Field
from sqlalchemy.ext.asyncio import AsyncSession

from src.observability.laminar import observe
from src.observability.langfuse_client import langfuse_observe
from src.services.linking import LinkedItem, find_related_content

@langfuse_observe(name="graph_rag_quality_service", as_type="span")
@observe(name="graph_rag_quality_service")
async def retrieve_graph_rag_context(
    session: AsyncSession,
    workspace_id: UUID,
    ticket: object,
    targets: list[RetrievalTarget] | None = None,

    requested_targets = targets or default_targets()

        workspace_id,
```

Implementation order:
1. Add typed retrieval/explanation contracts and deterministic eval fixtures.
2. Refactor `find_related_content()` so explicit references, hybrid search, graph traversal, and decision receipt lookup can be invoked by named target.
3. Make streaming and non-streaming context generation call the same target-aware retrieval path.
4. Persist compact explanation metadata in `context_page.sources` and suggestion payloads only after validators pass.

**Tool Use:**

- Do not introduce an agent/tool abstraction for this phase. The "tools" are trusted internal service calls: `hybrid_search()`, `traverse_graph()`, `generate_embedding()`, source lookups, existing `LLMClient.generate_json()`, and deterministic validators.
- Treat provider/source text as untrusted evidence, never as instructions. Prompt blocks should label source type, source ID, quote/snippet, URL, freshness state, and graph path explicitly.
- Keep model provider calls behind `LLMClient`; do not introduce LangChain chat-model adapters just to use `.with_structured_output()`.
- Trace service entry, target selection, retrieved source IDs, dropped source IDs/reasons, token counts, model name, prompt version, and eval case ID in existing Langfuse/Laminar spans.
- No external writes. Phase 46 may ground future action suggestions, but Phase 47 owns Slack/Telegram/Jira/Linear write confirmation flows.

**State Management:**

- Service-local state should stay compact: ticket identity, selected targets, `LinkedItem`/`RetrievalUnit` references, score features, validation notes, and explanation payloads.
- Durable state remains in existing Postgres/Supabase tables: graph nodes/edges, source records, context pages, missed-decision candidates, suggestions, and eval datasets.
- Do not add new checkpointing infrastructure for the first Phase 46 implementation. If a workflow must resume across process crashes, reuse existing Redis/Postgres infrastructure and require a stable run ID.
- If parallel retrieval branches are introduced later, keep shared SQLAlchemy session safety explicit; return target-specific result lists and merge them deterministically.

**Context Window Strategy:**

- Keep `CONTEXT_TOKEN_BUDGET` and `MAX_PROMPT_CHARS` as hard caps; add per-target budgets so graph neighborhoods cannot crowd out source evidence.
- Rank before truncation with explainable features: explicit reference, RRF score, graph depth/path, source freshness, workspace scope, verification state, source originality, and optional rerank score.
- Preserve exact source quotes/URLs for decisions and suggestions. If the full source text is too large, carry the quote plus a short surrounding snippet and source ID rather than stuffing the whole document into the prompt.
- Prefer primary source evidence over generated `context_page` graph nodes for factual grounding. Generated context can be navigational context, but should not outrank Slack/PR/meeting/ticket evidence unless explicitly requested.
- Streaming and non-streaming paths must share the same retrieval target policy; otherwise the UI can show weaker evidence than background generation.

### Structured Outputs with Pydantic

```python
from typing import Literal

from pydantic import BaseModel, Field, HttpUrl, ValidationError


class RetrievalSignalModel(BaseModel):
    kind: Literal[
        "explicit_reference",
        "hybrid_search",
        "graph_path",
        "freshness",
        "verification",
        "rrf",
        "llm_rerank",
    ]
    value: str
    score: float | None = Field(default=None, ge=0)


class RetrievalUnitAssessment(BaseModel):
    target: Literal[
        "decision_receipts",
        "source_evidence",
        "graph_neighborhood",
        "related_work",
    ]
    source_type: str
    source_id: str
    title: str
    url: HttpUrl | None = None
    quote: str | None = Field(default=None, min_length=1)
    graph_path: list[str] = Field(default_factory=list)
    freshness_state: Literal["current", "stale", "superseded", "syncing", "unknown"]
    verification_state: Literal[
        "confirmed_decision",
        "source_backed_candidate",
        "source_evidence",
        "generated_context",
        "unknown",
    ]
    signals: list[RetrievalSignalModel]
    why_selected: str = Field(min_length=12)
    suppress_reason: str | None = None
    confidence: float = Field(ge=0, le=1)


class GraphRagQualityAssessment(BaseModel):
    selected: list[RetrievalUnitAssessment]
    dropped: list[RetrievalUnitAssessment] = Field(default_factory=list)
    answer_grounded: bool
    validation_notes: list[str] = Field(default_factory=list)
```

The service layer should call the current provider-agnostic wrapper for structured validation:

```python
from uuid import UUID

from src.services.llm_client import get_llm_client


async def validate_graph_rag_packet(
    validator_prompt: str,
    system_prompt: str,
    workspace_id: UUID,
) -> GraphRagQualityAssessment:
    return await get_llm_client().generate_json(
        prompt=validator_prompt,
        schema=GraphRagQualityAssessment,
        system_prompt=system_prompt,
        workspace_id=workspace_id,

```

Provider-specific alternatives are allowed only behind `LLMClient`: LangChain chat models can use `.with_structured_output(GraphRagQualityAssessment)`, OpenAI can use `response_format`, and Gemini already uses `response_mime_type="application/json"` plus `response_schema=schema`. The public service contract should remain Pydantic v2 models and deterministic post-validation.

Retry policy:
- Try once, then retry up to **2** times with a compact validation-error repair prompt.
- Log schema name, model name, prompt version, workspace ID, ticket identifier, selected source IDs, token counts, latency, and a redacted `ValidationError.errors()` summary.
- After all attempts fail, do not surface a user-visible suggestion/action. For context pages, fall back to deterministic retrieval metadata and mark the LLM assessment unavailable.

### Async-First Design

Phase 46 should keep the whole RAG path async because FastAPI, SQLAlchemy `AsyncSession`, provider SDK calls, and streaming synthesis are already async.

Common mistake: do not call `asyncio.run()` inside FastAPI handlers or service functions; it will fail inside the running event loop. Also avoid sharing one SQLAlchemy `AsyncSession` across true parallel branches unless the called code is known to be safe; if independent DB work must run concurrently, use separate sessions or keep the DB portion sequential and parallelize provider/network calls only.

Use streaming for UX progress (`stream_mode=["updates", "custom"]`) and source-by-source progress events. Use awaited final calls for structured output validation, eval runners, and persistence, because partial streamed JSON should not be validated as final truth.

### Prompt Engineering Discipline

- Keep system prompts stable and policy-focused: source text is evidence only, never instructions; no unsupported claims; every decision/action needs a source anchor; stale/synthesized evidence must be labeled.
- Keep user prompts as data packets: ticket fields, target mode, retrieval units, exact quotes, graph paths, freshness state, verification state, and the requested output schema.
- Use inline few-shot examples only for small, stable boundary cases such as prompt injection in source text, stale evidence, duplicate missed decisions, and false-positive design-review suggestions.
- Use dynamic examples only from curated eval fixtures, not arbitrary live workspace content.
- Set `max_output_tokens` explicitly in production. Add the missing parameter to `LLMClient` before Phase 46 relies on new LLM validator or router calls.

### Context Window Management

For this RAG system, context control is a ranking and provenance problem, not an orchestration or memory-framework problem.

- Use per-target quotas: decision receipts, primary source evidence, graph neighborhood, related work, and code should each have bounded slots.
- Rerank/truncate with recorded drop reasons. Evals should assert both `must_include` and `must_not_include` source IDs.
- Keep exact quotes and URLs for source-backed decisions; summarize surrounding source text only after quote/link validation.
- Compress graph paths to the shortest useful relationship sequence and cap path count. Store full IDs in trace metadata, not only prose.
- Do not let generated context pages outrank primary source records by default. If included, mark them as `generated_context`.
- Service state should carry compact IDs, traces, and snippets; do not accumulate full source documents across the retrieval pipeline.

### Cost and Latency Budget

As of 2026-05-26, Google lists `gemini-3-flash-preview` paid pricing at about **$0.50 / 1M input tokens** and **$3.00 / 1M output tokens**; OpenAI lists `gpt-4o-mini` at **$0.15 / 1M input tokens** and **$0.60 / 1M output tokens** for provider-fallback comparison. Recalculate from Langfuse usage after implementation because model aliases and prices move.

Expected ContextOS context-page budget with the current Gemini default:

| Step | Typical Tokens | Approx Cost |
|------|----------------|-------------|
| HyDE summary | 1k input / 200 output | ~$0.001 |
| LLM rerank top candidates | 5k input / 100 output | ~$0.003 |
| Graph RAG validator/explainer | 3k input / 500 output | ~$0.003 |
| Final synthesis | 25k input / 2k output | ~$0.019 |
| **Total** | ~34k input / 2.8k output | **~$0.026 per generated page** |

Guardrails:
- Set a target p95 latency budget per context generation: retrieval <= 2s, rerank/validation <= 4s, synthesis <= 12s, total <= 20s excluding slow provider syncs.
- Cache exact-match sub-results by `(workspace_id, ticket_identifier, source_sync_version, model_name, prompt_version)`.
- Add semantic caching only for low-risk sub-tasks such as HyDE/query expansion and route classification, never for final source-grounded answers without checking source versions.
- Use cheaper configured models for routing, duplicate classification, freshness summarisation, and eval-judge experiments. Keep final synthesis and user-visible suggestion grounding on the standard configured model until evals prove a cheaper model is safe.
- Track cost in Langfuse/Laminar per node: HyDE, hybrid search embedding, rerank, validation, synthesis, and retries.

## 5. Evaluation Strategy

Phase 46 answers "is Graph RAG better?" through deterministic product evals first, tracing second, and calibrated human/LLM review only where code cannot judge narrative quality. The critical failure modes under test are unsupported decision evidence, stale evidence outranking fresher source-backed evidence, workspace/source-boundary leakage, ungrounded action suggestions, and quality claims without repeatable fixtures.

### Dimensions

| Dimension | Rubric | Measurement Approach | Priority |
|-----------|--------|----------------------|----------|
| Decision recall and target coverage | PASS: For a labeled ticket/query, retrieval includes the expected Decision Receipt or source-backed missed-decision candidate in top K and records the target that found it (`decision_receipts`, `source_evidence`, `graph_neighborhood`, or `related_work`).<br>FAIL: The expected decision is absent, hidden behind unrelated related work, or only appears through synthesized context when primary evidence exists. | Code: `recall@k`, expected decision/source ID set match in `backend/src/evals/graph_rag.py`.<br>Human: senior engineer reviews disputed expected labels. | Critical |
| Source-link accuracy and citation validity | PASS: Every cited decision/action source has exact `source_type`, `source_id`, stable URL/link, and required quote/snippet; missed-decision candidates preserve exact `source_quote` and valid `source_link` through suggestion payloads.<br>FAIL: A generated context page, loose graph neighbor, broken URL, or non-substring quote is treated as primary evidence. | Code: URL/source ID exact match, quote substring validation, required field checks, Pydantic schema validation.<br>Human: sample source-owner review for ambiguous provider links. | Critical |
| Context faithfulness and unsupported-claim control | PASS: Context page prose and suggestion explanations only assert decisions, owners, affected systems, risks, and actions present in selected retrieval units or confirmed Decision metadata; uncertainty and missing evidence are explicit.<br>FAIL: The answer invents rationale, owners, actionability, or "the team decided" claims not grounded in selected evidence. | Code: required source anchor per decision/action claim where structured metadata exists.<br>LLM Judge: sampled faithfulness judge compares final prose against retrieval packet after calibration against human labels.<br>Human: edge-case review for conflicting evidence. | Critical |
| Stale-context avoidance and supersession | PASS: Current confirmed Decisions, recent source records, and source-backed promoted candidates outrank stale/superseded/syncing/generated context unless the query explicitly asks for history; stale evidence is labeled when included.<br>FAIL: Old Slack/docs/context pages override newer PR/spec/Decision evidence without a stale or superseded warning. | Code: fixture-level `must_not_rank_above`, freshness state assertions, stale warning presence, generated-context downranking checks. | High |
| Workspace isolation and access parity | PASS: Every retrieval unit, graph path, trace, citation, context source, candidate, and suggestion belongs to the active workspace and allowed source state; workspace scope is a hard filter, not a ranking feature.<br>FAIL: Any result comes from another workspace, disconnected integration, soft-deleted source, or source state the current workspace cannot view. | Code: workspace ID equality assertions, negative cross-workspace fixtures, source-state validators.<br>Human: workspace admin/security owner reviews boundary fixtures. | Critical |
| Provenance completeness and explainability | PASS: Selected and dropped retrieval units include target, signal list, score features, graph path where applicable, freshness state, verification state, `why_selected` or `suppress_reason`, model/prompt version, and eval case ID when running fixtures.<br>FAIL: A source appears in `context_page.sources` or a suggestion payload with only title/score/URL and no reason it was selected or suppressed. | Code: Pydantic required fields, trace metadata checks, selected/dropped source ID set checks. | Critical |
| Action-suggestion grounding and no-write boundary | PASS: Future action suggestions are grounded in a confirmed Decision or source-backed candidate, name a concrete target/reason/owner when available, preserve the evidence trail, and make clear that Phase 46 performs no external write.<br>FAIL: Generic "create a task", "update docs", or "follow up" text appears from similarity/adjacency alone, lacks source anchors, or implies Slack/Jira/Linear writes before Phase 47 confirmation. | Code: required evidence fields, allowed suggestion type checks, no external-write action assertion.<br>Human: product/design lead samples actionability and priority labels. | High |
| Noise control and retrieval precision | PASS: Weak, duplicate, already-resolved, already-dismissed, low-actionability, prompt-injected, or ambiguous candidates are suppressed with a reason; fewer high-confidence Needs review items beat broad coverage.<br>FAIL: Routine chatter, stale graph neighbors, duplicate candidates, or missing-source false positives create user-visible suggestions. | Code: precision@k, false-positive fixture rate, duplicate/suppression reason checks, existing missed-decision precision/recall runner.<br>Human: reviewer fatigue sample on Needs review items. | High |
| Stream/non-stream parity | PASS: Streaming and non-stream context generation use the same target-aware retrieval policy or record an explicit route omission reason; graph/source evidence sets are equal for deterministic fixtures.<br>FAIL: The UI stream omits graph evidence or uses weaker scoring while background generation includes stronger evidence. | Code: fixture-level source ID set equality or explained-difference assertion. | High |
| Safety: source-text injection and data minimization | PASS: Slack/docs/tickets/source text is treated only as evidence, not instructions; prompt-injection strings do not create candidates/suggestions/actions; traces avoid leaking raw provider text beyond the workspace. <br>FAIL: Source text changes system behavior, bypasses validators, leaks cross-workspace data, or produces an unsafe external-action instruction. | Code: adversarial fixture checks, workspace filters, trace redaction checks.<br>LLM Judge/Human: sampled safety review for novel prompt-injection variants. | Critical |

**Primary Tool:** Existing deterministic Python eval runners in `backend/src/evals`, plus existing Langfuse and Laminar tracing for debugging and production observability.

Do **not** add Arize Phoenix, RAGAS, Promptfoo, or LangSmith for Phase 46 unless deterministic Graph RAG fixtures prove insufficient. ### Reference Dataset

**Size:** Start with 20 production-quality JSONL examples before Phase 46 is considered planned for execution. A 10-case bootstrap is acceptable only for the first implementation wave, but the phase gate requires 20.

**Composition:**

- 4 confirmed Decision recall cases across Slack/docs/PR/ticket evidence, including expected `must_include_decision_ids`.
- 3 source-link accuracy cases with exact quote/link requirements and one intentionally hallucinated quote negative.
- 3 stale/supersession cases where old Slack/docs/context-page evidence must be downranked or labeled.
- 3 workspace/source-boundary adversarial cases, including cross-workspace graph nodes and disconnected/soft-deleted sources.
- 3 action-suggestion grounding cases covering missed-decision candidate to suggestion payloads, action hints, and the no-write Phase 46 boundary.
- 2 noise-control cases for duplicate, already-dismissed, weak-evidence, or generic missing-source suggestions.
- 2 stream/non-stream parity and synthesized-context downranking cases.

**Labeling:**

Automated labels define expected source IDs, decision IDs, must-include/must-not-include sets, source links, quote substrings, freshness state, verification state, and required explanation fields. A senior engineer/tech lead labels decision recall and implementation relevance; a product/design lead labels actionability and missing-source usefulness; a workspace admin/security owner labels isolation and prompt-injection cases. LLM judges may be added only for sampled prose faithfulness after their scores reach at least 0.70 correlation with human labels on the reference set.

Create the dataset during Wave 0, before ranking or prompt tuning. Every new production failure should add or update one fixture before the ranking/prompt change that fixes it.

## 6. Guardrails

### Online (Real-Time)

| Guardrail | Trigger | Intervention |
|-----------|---------|--------------|
| Workspace/source hard filter | Any retrieved item, graph path, citation, trace row, candidate, or suggestion has a different `workspace_id`, disconnected source state, soft-deleted source, or invalid source visibility. | Drop the item, mark the run invalid, log a Critical trace event, and block user-visible suggestion/action output for that run. |
| Source provenance validator | A decision/action claim lacks `source_type`, `source_id`, URL/link, required quote/snippet, verification state, or exact quote validation where the surface requires source-backed evidence. | Suppress the suggestion/action; for context pages, omit the unsupported claim or show deterministic retrieval metadata with a missing-provenance warning. |
| Freshness and synthesized-context gate | Stale/superseded/syncing/unknown-freshness evidence or `generated_context` would outrank current primary source evidence without an explicit historical query. | Downrank or drop from grounding; if retained as context, label freshness and prevent it from grounding action suggestions. |
| Source-text injection guard | Retrieved source text contains instructions to ignore policies, create actions, expose secrets, or change ranking/output behavior. | Treat as quoted evidence only, never instructions; block candidate/suggestion creation if the only support is injected text. |
| Action no-write boundary | Output implies posting, creating, sending, updating, or assigning work in Slack/Telegram/Jira/Linear/docs before Phase 47 confirmation flow exists. | Block or rewrite as "review suggested follow-up" with evidence only; never call external write paths in Phase 46. |
| Token/latency budget guard | Retrieval, rerank, validation, or synthesis exceeds configured token caps or p95 latency budget for context generation. | Stop optional LLM validation/rerank first, fall back to deterministic retrieval metadata, and flag trace for offline review. |

### Offline (Flywheel)

| Metric | Sampling Strategy | Action on Degradation |
|--------|------------------|----------------------|
| Decision recall@5 and target coverage | Run full golden JSONL set in CI; nightly live replay on recent context-page generations once runner exists. | Add failing fixture, inspect selected/dropped source traces, adjust target routing or ranking features before prompt changes. |
| Source-link exact-match rate | 100% of deterministic cases; sample production context pages and suggestions with source-backed decisions. | Fix source extraction/link preservation; block release if exact-match drops below threshold. |
| Context faithfulness / unsupported claim rate | Sample generated context pages where synthesis produced `[DECISION]`, `[ACTION]`, or suggestion explanations; oversample low-confidence and stale-warning traces. | Add anchor requirements or post-validator; calibrate LLM judge against human review before using as a gate. |
| Stale/supersession violation rate | Sample traces containing stale, superseded, generated-context, or unknown-freshness evidence. | Update provider freshness matrix, ranking feature weights, and stale labeling fixtures. |
| Workspace isolation failures | 100% online guardrail failures plus adversarial fixture runs. | Treat any non-zero rate as release-blocking; audit query predicates and caches. |
| Noise false-positive rate | Sample Needs review items by suggestion family, including dismissed/snoozed/reopened outcomes. | Tighten suppress reasons, duplicate matching, source thresholds, and suggestion-family policy blocks. |
| Action-grounding pass rate | Sample future action-suggestion payloads and all Phase 45 candidate-to-suggestion bridge changes. | Suppress generic action hints; require confirmed Decision/source-backed candidate evidence before surfacing. |
| Signal-metric divergence | Compare eval scores with user behavior signals: rapid dismissals, repeated regenerations, source-chip clicks, and manual correction patterns. | Open a new fixture for the divergent case and review with the relevant domain expert role. |

## 7. Production Monitoring

**Tracing Tool:** Existing Langfuse + Laminar. Keep decorators centralized through `langfuse_observe`, `observe`, `update_generation`, and `set_laminar_metadata`; add Graph RAG spans around target selection, retrieval, feature building, ranking, validation, synthesis, and suggestion grounding.

**Key Metrics to Track:**

- `graph_rag_eval_pass`: deterministic Graph RAG eval runner pass/fail, plus case-level recall, precision, stale violation, source-link accuracy, provenance completeness, and action-grounding fields.
- Retrieval quality: `decision_recall_at_5`, `retrieval_precision_at_k`, expected source/decision ID misses, selected vs dropped source counts, and per-target contribution.
- Provenance quality: source-link exact-match rate, quote validation rate, missing `why_selected` / `suppress_reason` rate, graph path presence for graph-derived results.
- Safety and tenancy: workspace mismatch count, disconnected/soft-deleted source count, source-text injection suppressions, generated-context grounding attempts.
- Freshness and verification: stale/superseded evidence selected count, confirmed Decision vs candidate vs raw source vs generated-context ranking distribution.
- Action/noise quality: grounded action-suggestion pass rate, generic-action suppression count, duplicate/already-dismissed suppression count, Needs review dismissal/reopen/resolution rates.
- Operations: p95 context-generation latency <= 20s, retrieval <= 2s, rerank/validation <= 4s, synthesis <= 12s, token/cost per node, retry count, provider/model version.

**Alert Thresholds:**

- Page/block release immediately: any workspace isolation failure, cross-workspace trace/citation, invalid source visibility leak, or external write attempt from Phase 46 code.
- Block merge/release: Graph RAG deterministic eval fails; `decision_recall_at_5 < 0.85`; source-link exact-match `< 0.98`; provenance completeness `< 0.95`; stale violation rate `> 0.02`; action-grounding pass rate `< 0.90`; noise false-positive rate `> 0.10`.
- Investigate within one business day: unsupported-claim sampled rate `> 0.01`, generated-context grounding attempts increase week over week, p95 total context generation `> 20s`, rerank/validation p95 `> 4s`, or per-page estimated LLM cost doubles from baseline.
- Track but do not page: LLM judge/human disagreement above calibration target, source-chip click-through changes, or dismissal/reopen trend shifts unless they correlate with eval regression.

**Smart Sampling Strategy:**

Trace 100% of Graph RAG runs with redacted metadata: workspace ID, ticket identifier, retrieval targets, selected/dropped source IDs, signals, graph path IDs, freshness state, verification state, score features, prompt/model version, token counts, latency, guardrail outcomes, and eval case ID when present.

Human/LLM review sampling should be weighted, not random: sample 100% of guardrail failures and eval failures; 25% of user-visible suggestions/action prompts; 10% of context pages with `[DECISION]` or `[ACTION]` sections; and oversample runs with stale warnings, graph depth > 2, generated-context candidates, missing quotes, low confidence, high token use, retries, rapid dismissals, repeated regeneration, or source conflicts. Store the reviewed trace link with the fixture or remediation task so production failures feed the offline eval corpus.
