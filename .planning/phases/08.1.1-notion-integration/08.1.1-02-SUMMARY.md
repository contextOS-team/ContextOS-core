## Decisions Made
- Used NOTION_WEBHOOK_SECRET as a single env var for HMAC verification (same pattern as LINEAR_WEBHOOK_SECRET, GITHUB_WEBHOOK_SECRET)
- Derived delivery_id from SHA256 hash of event_type:entity_id:timestamp since Notion webhooks do not provide a delivery ID header
- Webhook verification flow (initial setup) returns {status: "verified"} for handshake
- sync_page_by_id creates its own session for background task isolation (same pattern as other webhook services)
